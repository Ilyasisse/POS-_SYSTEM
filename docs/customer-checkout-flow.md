# Customer checkout and fulfillment

Customers can browse `/customer` and build a cart without signing in. Checkout asks a guest to continue with Google. The cart and dine-in/to-go/delivery selection are saved in session storage before leaving and restored against the current catalog after returning. Delivery address and contact are restored along with the selection. No payment is submitted automatically after sign-in. The name defaults to the verified customer's stored Google profile name. Customer notes are removed. Payment phone input is required: exactly `90` and seven digits, without `252`. Server-side normalization stores the full number for receipt matching.

Before payment the customer chooses to-go, an active dine-in table, or delivery. Delivery requires an address of 5–500 characters and a contact phone with at least five digits and at most 30 characters. The delivery contact can differ from the payment phone; receipt matching always uses the payment phone. Delivery has no dine-in table. Android Pay Now uses a dialer link for `*884*430935*AMOUNT#`. Other devices can copy the code. The customer authorizes the payment by calling and entering a PIN in their phone app. The web app never collects a PIN. Existing MacroDroid forwarding must send authenticated SAHAL SMS messages to `/api/webhooks/payments/macrodroid`; opening the dialer alone is not proof of payment.

Automatic payment matching requires an incoming GOLIS receipt, the same normalized payer phone and cent amount, exactly one eligible checkout, a unique unassigned provider reference, and receipt/payment processing within the checkout's 15-minute window. The existing one-second tolerance accounts for SMS timestamps with second precision. Row locks and unique constraints prevent reuse. Outgoing messages cannot pay for an order. Unmatched incoming receipts remain in the review inbox.

Admin and manager payment review is at `/admin/payment-review`. It includes unresolved checkouts, unassigned incoming receipts, malformed SMS correction, and paid-order handover. A manual assignment needs an audit reason. Cashiers can assign exact phone/time matches; admin/manager review may resolve phone/time exceptions with a reason. Amount differences, reused references, unavailable receipts, and payments predating the checkout remain blocked. Receipt correction and assignment retain audit records. Unresolved old checkouts and receipts are included instead of disappearing at midnight.

Paid customer orders persist their dine-in table/to-go/delivery choice and create kitchen ticket state in the same transaction. Delivery address and contact are saved on the checkout before payment and copied to the confirmed order. The confirmation page, admin orders, and staff fulfillment board show these delivery details, and staff assignment notifications identify the delivery destination. A waiter with an open shift for the current business day receives a durable order notification. An available cashier with a browser heartbeat within 90 seconds is assigned for fallback/support. If neither is available, the order remains on the paid fulfillment board and admin cashier-status page for staff to handle. Notifications are displayed in the staff shell and stored as order assignment audit events. They are not external push messages.

The checkout URL keeps polling every three seconds while visible and refreshes on return from the dialer. It reads persisted kitchen and pickup state: payment, kitchen received, preparing, ready, picked up, delivered. It stays on that page after payment and redirects to the menu five seconds after confirmed delivery. Staff mark delivery/handover on the existing waiter board or customer fulfillment board. No browser-back interception is added; explicit navigation away still works.

Cashiers select Available, Busy, Away, or Offline in the staff header. Each actual switch is audited. Heartbeats do not create repeated audit rows. `/admin/cashier-status` shows the selected status, effective online/offline state, last heartbeat, status audit history, and orders awaiting staff assignment.

## Rollout and verification

Apply the Prisma migration before deploying the new app:
`npx prisma migrate deploy`, then regenerate Prisma during the build. This includes the order delivery columns and the later checkout delivery columns; existing to-go and dine-in checkouts keep null delivery details. Legacy checkouts default to to-go and cashiers initially default to offline. No live database changes are made by this PR.

The customer checkout workflow runs phone/payment/progress/draft regression tests, payment parser tests, PGlite migration tests, Prisma validation and TypeScript checking. This task's execution environment failed provisioning, so browser verification and real Google/dialer/SMS integration must be checked in staging. Use two different payer phones, wrong amounts, late receipts, duplicate SMS, simultaneous receipt claims, dine-in and to-go orders, and return-from-dialer navigation.
